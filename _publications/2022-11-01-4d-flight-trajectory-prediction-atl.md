A 4D trajectory has three spatial dimensions and time, and each of them carries its own uncertainty. In this paper, we proposed CG3D, a hybrid model in which a CNN extracts the spatial features, a GRU extracts the temporal features, and a 3D-CNN extracts both of them together, and we used Monte Carlo dropout to account for the uncertainty of the prediction. The model was trained and tested on the ADS-B data of the OpenSky Network for the flights of Hartsfield–Jackson Atlanta International Airport.

Published as Hesam Shafienya, with Dr. Amelia C. Regan (University of California, Irvine).
