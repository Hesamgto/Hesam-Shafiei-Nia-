This report summarizes the policy and practice of zero-emission delivery in US cities as of January 2025, including the electrification of delivery vehicles, the shift to smaller forms such as e-bikes, and the management of city space. It provides a baseline for future work and the levers that US cities can use.

Urban Freight Lab, University of Washington.
