To predict a 4D trajectory accurately, the spatial and the temporal features of a flight have to be extracted at the same time. In this paper, we compared hybrid CNN-GRU models in which a CNN extracts the spatial features and a GRU extracts the temporal features, and we tested whether giving the two parts separate inputs is better than one unified input. The models were trained on the historical ADS-B data of the OpenSky Network.

Published as Hesam Shafienya, with Dr. Amelia Regan (University of California, Irvine).
