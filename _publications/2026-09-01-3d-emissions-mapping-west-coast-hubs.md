ADS-B data is open and detailed, but many flights have missing segments, and in the January 2025 data of the three west coast hubs, more than half of the flights needed imputation. In this paper, we filled the gaps of the trajectories with an autoencoder, estimated the fuel burn and the emissions along each flight with the BADA aircraft performance model, and mapped the emissions and their social cost by location and altitude.

With Dr. Don MacKenzie (University of Washington).
