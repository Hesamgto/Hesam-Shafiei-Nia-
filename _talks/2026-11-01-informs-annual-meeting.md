A framework that generates plausible alternative flight trajectories with a conditional variational autoencoder (VampPrior), estimates their fuel burn and emissions with BADA, and shows the trade-off between the airline cost and the external cost of each flight as a Pareto frontier to support dispatchers. With Dr. Don MacKenzie.
